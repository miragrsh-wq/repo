Taylor Swift

Name: Taylor Alison Swift

Born: December 13, 1989

Profession: Singer, songwriter, actress

Genres: Pop, country, indie

Known for: Multiple Grammy Awards, worldwide tours, and influential songwriting

Biography:

Taylor Swift is an American singer and songwriter, born on December 13, 1989. She began her career in country music and became one of the most successful artists worldwide. Known for her storytelling lyrics and record-breaking albums, she has won multiple Grammy Awards and sold out stadium tours. In 2023, she was named Time Magazine’s Person of the Year, highlighting her influence on global music and culture.
